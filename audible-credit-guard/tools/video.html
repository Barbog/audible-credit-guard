Credit Guard for Audible

Know before your credits overflow.

Audible caps the credits you can hold.

When a new credit lands, anything above the cap is simply gone. Audible doesn't warn you before, and doesn't tell you after.

One check a month. Two alerts at most.

It reads your balance the day after a credit lands, while there's still a month to act, and reminds you once more two days before the next one — only if you're still at the cap.

One click into Audible's own pause dialog.

Pausing stops accrual and billing, so nothing overflows. The extension opens the dialog and highlights the control. You confirm — it never does that for you.
